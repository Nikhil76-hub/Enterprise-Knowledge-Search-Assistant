# Acme Corp Health Insurance and Benefits FAQ

## Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage
Acme Corp provides comprehensive medical coverage through Blue Shield PPO and Kaiser Permanente HMO. The company subsidizes 85% of employee monthly premiums and 75% of eligible dependent premiums. Dental insurance is administered through Delta Dental with a $2,500 annual maximum coverage limit per individual. Vision care is provided by VSP, featuring a $10 copayment for annual eye examinations and a $200 hardware frame allowance every 12 months.

## HSA and FSA Contribution Limits
Employees enrolled in the high-deductible Blue Shield PPO qualify for a Health Savings Account (HSA). Acme contributes $1,000 annually for individual coverage or $2,000 for family coverage, paid out in quarterly installments on the first payroll of each quarter. Flexible Spending Accounts (Healthcare FSA) allow pre-tax contributions up to $3,200 annually, with a $640 rollover limit into the next calendar year.

## Open Enrollment and Qualifying Life Events
Annual Open Enrollment occurs from November 1 through November 21, with benefit elections taking effect on January 1. Changes outside Open Enrollment require a Qualifying Life Event (QLE), such as marriage, birth, or loss of existing coverage, and must be submitted via Workday within 30 calendar days of the event.
