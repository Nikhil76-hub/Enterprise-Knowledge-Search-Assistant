# Acme Corp Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility and Work Schedules
Full-time employees who have completed their initial 90-day probationary period are eligible for hybrid remote work upon manager approval. Eligible staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as mandatory in-office collaboration days. All employees must maintain core working hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST, regardless of work location, to facilitate cross-functional meetings and team availability.

## Equipment Stipend and Monthly Internet Allowance
Acme Corp provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $500, claimable within the first 30 days of employment for desk accessories, monitors, or ergonomic chairs. In addition, remote-eligible employees receive a recurring monthly internet reimbursement of $60, automatically credited via payroll on the 25th of each month. Receipts for the initial $500 stipend must be filed through Expensify under the "Home Office" expense category.

## Workspace Security and Environment Standards
Employees working remotely must ensure confidentiality of corporate data. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. When working from public locations such as cafes or hotels, connecting through Acme's GlobalProtect VPN is strictly mandatory, and shoulder-surfing privacy filters must be used. For home office hardware support or remote policy questions, contact Workplace Operations at workplace-ops@acmecorp.com, internal extension 4020, or Slack channel #remote-work.
