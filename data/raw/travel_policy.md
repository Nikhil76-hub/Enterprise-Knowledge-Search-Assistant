# Acme Corp Corporate Travel and Entertainment Policy

## Booking Procedures and Airfare Tiers
All business-related travel must be booked through Navan (formerly TripActions) at least 14 calendar days in advance for domestic travel and 21 calendar days for international travel. Economy class is standard for all flights under 6 continuous hours. Flights exceeding 6 hours qualify for Premium Economy, while continuous international flights exceeding 8 hours qualify for Business Class. Department Director approval is mandatory prior to ticketing any international itinerary.

## Lodging and Ground Transportation Rules
Hotel nightly room rates are capped at $250 per night in standard cities and $350 per night in designated high-cost metropolitan markets (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich). Ride-share services (Uber, Lyft) are authorized for airport, hotel, and client site transit up to $65 per single trip. Personal vehicle usage for corporate travel is reimbursed at the federal IRS standard rate of $0.67 per mile. Daily incidental expenses (tips, laundry for stays > 4 nights) are capped at $20 per day.
