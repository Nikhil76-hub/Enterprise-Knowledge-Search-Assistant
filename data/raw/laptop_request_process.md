# Acme Corp Laptop and Hardware Provisioning Process

## Standard Hardware Profiles
Acme Corp provisions standardized hardware based on role specifications. Engineering, Product Design, and Data Science personnel receive an Apple MacBook Pro 16-inch (M3 Pro, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD) or a Dell Precision 5680 (32GB RAM, 1TB SSD). All other departments receive an Apple MacBook Air 14-inch (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) or a Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 4 (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD). Peripheral bundles include one 27-inch 4K monitor and an ergonomic wireless keyboard and mouse.

## Ordering Workflow and Approval Levels
Hardware requests must be submitted through the Jira Service Desk portal at jira.acmecorp.com/servicedesk under project queue "HWREQ". Standard role replacements require direct manager approval. Custom hardware configurations or upgrades exceeding $2,500 require additional authorization from the department Vice President. Requests for secondary monitors or specialized accessories require Director sign-off.

## Replacement Cycles and Delivery Timelines
Laptops operate on a mandatory 36-month refresh cycle. IT Asset Management issues refresh notifications 30 days prior to eligibility. Domestic hardware shipments are dispatched via FedEx Ground within 3 to 5 business days of approval. International requests require 10 to 14 business days for customs clearance. For urgent shipping status updates, contact IT Asset Depot at it-assets@acmecorp.com or call internal extension 3150.
