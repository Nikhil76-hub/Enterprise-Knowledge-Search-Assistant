# Acme Corp New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Pre-Arrival and Day 1 Milestones
New hires must activate their Acme Okta credentials by 9:00 AM EST on their designated start date. Virtual New Hire Orientation begins promptly at 10:30 AM EST via Zoom link provided in your welcome package. Under federal regulations, Section 2 of Form I-9 document verification must be completed within 72 hours (3 business days) of your first working day. Direct deposit instructions and tax withholding forms must be submitted in Workday by 5:00 PM on Day 1.

## Week 1 Training Requirements
During your first 5 business days, you must complete 4 mandatory compliance courses on Acme Academy: Information Security Fundamentals (45 minutes), Preventing Workplace Harassment (60 minutes), Insider Trading Regulations (30 minutes), and Data Privacy & GDPR Standards (45 minutes). Employees must also schedule introductory 1-on-1 meetings with their manager and designated onboarding buddy before Friday at 4:00 PM EST.

## 30-60-90 Day Review Milestones
At Day 30, employees collaborate with their manager in Lattice to finalize individual OKRs and professional goals. At Day 60, a mid-probation progress check is conducted to evaluate role onboarding. At Day 90, the formal probationary assessment concludes, transitioning the employee to regular permanent status. For onboarding support, reach out to onboarding@acmecorp.com, call +1-800-555-0112, or join Slack #welcome-newbies.
