# Acme Corp Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

## Workplace Professionalism and Harassment Prevention
Acme Corp is dedicated to maintaining a safe, inclusive, and professional workplace. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, and retaliatory conduct based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or religion are strictly prohibited. All employees must complete annual anti-harassment training (60 minutes) by October 31. Violations of this policy will result in immediate disciplinary measures up to and including employment termination.

## Conflicts of Interest and Vendor Gift Limits
Employees must avoid situations where personal or financial relationships conflict with Acme Corp interests. Receiving gifts, meals, or entertainment from suppliers, contractors, or competitors exceeding $50 in estimated value is prohibited without written compliance approval. Gifts exceeding this threshold must be logged in the Compliance Gift Register within 7 business days. Cash gifts or equivalents (gift cards) of any denomination are strictly prohibited under all circumstances.

## Whistleblower Protection and Reporting Hotlines
Suspected violations of law or corporate policy should be reported promptly. Acme maintains a strict non-retaliation policy for good-faith reports. Reports can be submitted 24/7 anonymously through the EthicsPoint hotline at 1-888-555-0177 or online at ethics.acmecorp.com. Investigations are initiated by the Legal & Compliance Department within 48 hours of report submission. For direct inquiries, email compliance@acmecorp.com or reach the Chief Compliance Officer at extension 2100.
