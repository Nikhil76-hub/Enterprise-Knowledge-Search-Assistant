# Acme Corp Business Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Allowable Expenses and Per Diem Limits
Acme Corp reimburses reasonable and necessary business travel and operational expenses. Daily meal per diem is capped at $75 per day during authorized travel, allocated as $15 for breakfast, $25 for lunch, and $35 for dinner. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner. Client entertainment expenses exceeding $150 per event require advance written approval from a department director prior to the event.

## Documentation and Submission Deadlines
All expense claims must be filed through Expensify within 30 calendar days of incurring the expense. Itemized receipts showing date, vendor name, item description, and payment method are mandatory for any expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary charge slips and bank statements are not acceptable documentation. Claims submitted after 60 calendar days from the transaction date are deemed untimely and will be rejected.

## Approval Hierarchy and Payment Schedule
Direct managers must review and approve expense reports within 5 business days of submission. Finance conducts secondary audits on all reports over $1,000 within 3 business days. Approved reimbursements are paid out bi-weekly on the 15th and final business day of each month via direct deposit. For reimbursement discrepancies or audit inquiries, contact Accounts Payable at ap-expenses@acmecorp.com or post in Slack channel #finance-support.
