# Acme Corp Employee Leave and Time-Off Policy

## Paid Time Off Accrual and Rollover
Full-time Acme Corp employees accrue 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year at the rate of 1.67 days per month worked. Part-time employees scheduled for at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis. A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, but these rolled-over days must be used by March 31. Any remaining rolled-over days beyond this deadline, or unused days in excess of the 5-day rollover limit, are forfeited without cash payout.

## Sick and Bereavement Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days annually, credited on January 1. Sick leave does not roll over. If an employee is absent due to illness for more than 3 consecutive business days, a signed medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider must be submitted to People Operations within 48 hours of return. Acme Corp also provides up to 5 consecutive paid business days for bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member.

## Submission Deadlines and Approval Process
Planned vacation requests of 3 consecutive days or more must be submitted through Workday at least 14 calendar days in advance. Single-day time-off requests require at least 24 hours prior notice. In unforeseen emergencies, employees must notify their direct manager and email hr-benefits@acmecorp.com by 8:00 AM on the day of absence. Managers must review and approve all leave requests within 3 business days. For urgent inquiries, contact the People Operations Helpdesk at +1-800-555-0144 or via Slack at #ask-hr.
